# Etch A Sketch
Your very own Etch A Sketch on your browser! Drag your mouse over the canvas to draw.
![](attachments/Website%20screenshot.png)

This was originally made for [The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com/)'s Foundations course. After finishing it, I wanted to embellish this project; make it fun, not just functional!
